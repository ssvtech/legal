# iChits Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 September 2026
**Last updated:** 15 August 2026
**Operator:** SSV Creative Labs, Himayath Nagar, Hyderabad, Telangana, India
**Contact:** ssvcreativelabs@gmail.com

iChits is operated by **SSV Creative Labs** (we, us, our). This policy explains how iChits collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the mobile app. iChits is a private chit record-keeping app. It does not collect, hold, transfer, process, or facilitate payments.

## 1. Information we collect

We collect account email and email-authentication details; profile name and optional photo; chit names, schedules, contributions, turns, manual payment records, notes, and statuses; uploaded screenshots, receipts, and documents; invitation names and emails; optional notification and camera/gallery/file permissions; technical diagnostics used for support; optional, anonymous crash and ANR reports when you enable them; and optional encrypted backup data and metadata.

We do not collect bank passwords, card PINs, CVVs, banking OTPs, bank-account credentials, or payment credentials. iChits does not currently request Contacts permission; if contact selection is introduced, it will be optional and manual entry will remain available.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to secure accounts, display private records and schedules, deliver invitations and relevant notifications, synchronize signed-in devices, provide optional backup and restore, respond to support requests, improve reliability, prevent misuse and security incidents, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data or use chit records, attachments, or contact information for advertising.

## 3. Who can see information

- Members see their own profile, records, attachments, due status, own turns, and non-personal schedule information.
- Members cannot see another member’s email, photo, contact details, proof, record, notes, or personal details.
- Organizers manage member details and records only for chits they organize.
- Super Admin/Product Owner can access account and platform-support metadata. Private chit records and attachments are inaccessible by default; support access needs explicit, scoped, time-limited consent and immutable audit logs.

Organizers are responsible for having a valid basis to enter or invite a person’s name and email.

## 4. Sharing

We share information only with authorized users in the same chit under these role rules, service providers delivering authentication, hosting, storage, notifications, backup, opted-in crash diagnostics, or support, authorities where law requires, and in a business transaction subject to safeguards. We do not publicly disclose personal or financial information.

## 5. Permissions

Notifications are used for reminders, invitation updates, record updates, turns, backups, and security alerts. Camera, gallery, or files are used only when you choose an attachment. You can decline optional permissions, revoke them in device settings, and use available manual alternatives.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including secure transmission, role-based access controls, private storage policies, and audit logging for sensitive support access. No electronic system is completely secure; protect your email account, device, iChits PIN, and backup account.

## 7. Backup and third parties

Google backup is optional. When enabled, iChits creates an encrypted backup in the selected Google account; Google’s terms and privacy practices also apply. You can disable backup in iChits Settings. Providers process information only to deliver their services under appropriate safeguards.

## 8. Retention and deletion

We retain information while an account is active or as needed to provide iChits, preserve a shared record, resolve disputes, prevent misuse, or comply with law. You may export data and request correction or deletion through the app or by email. Current account deletion is permanent when eligible; it is refused while active memberships, organizer chits, or retained audit records remain. We do not offer a recovery period. Support records, audit records, and encrypted backups are retained for up to 30 days after deletion or closure, unless law requires longer.

## 8a. Subscription billing, cancellation and refunds

The optional iChits subscription is billed for platform access only — never for chit money. Cancelling stops future renewal; access continues until the period you already paid for ends, and your chit data is never affected by a plan change or cancellation.

While iChits payment collection is manual (a bank/UPI transfer confirmed by our support team against a support ticket, pending Razorpay going live), refunds are handled the same way: raise a support ticket describing the issue within 7 days of the charge, and we will review it case by case. A wrongly charged or duplicate payment is refunded in full; a change-of-mind request after meaningful use of the paid plan in that period is not guaranteed. Once card/UPI payments go live through Razorpay, this section will be updated with the provider's own dispute and chargeback handling.

## 9. Your choices and rights

You may update profile information; manage permissions, language, theme, reminders, backup, and app lock; export data; withdraw optional consent; request deletion; and raise a privacy concern. India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 addresses notice, consent, access, correction/erasure, and grievance redressal rights. See the [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/22037?col=123456789%2F1362&view_type=search).

## 10. Children

iChits is not intended for children. Do not use it below the minimum age required to consent to data processing. Contact us if you believe a child supplied personal information.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the update in the app and change the Last updated date. Material changes receive additional notice where required.

## 12. Contact and grievance

For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact **SSV Creative Labs** at **ssvcreativelabs@gmail.com**. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

For Google Play data-disclosure requirements, see the [Google Play User Data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311).
