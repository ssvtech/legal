# Delete Your iChits Account

**Operator:** SSV Creative Labs
**Contact:** ssvcreativelabs@gmail.com

## Delete in the app (fastest)

1. Open iChits and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings > Privacy**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.

Your account is deleted immediately if you have no active chit memberships, no chits you organize, and no retained audit records. If deletion is blocked, the app tells you what to resolve first (for example, leaving an active chit).

## Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email **ssvcreativelabs@gmail.com** from the email address on your iChits account and ask us to delete your account. We will verify your request and process it within 30 days.

## What gets deleted

Your profile, email, chit records you organize, your own payment and proof records, and your notification settings are permanently deleted once eligible. Deletion cannot be undone and there is no recovery period.

## What may be retained, and for how long

Support tickets, audit/compliance records, and encrypted backups may be retained for up to 30 days after deletion, unless a longer period is required by law. See the full [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html) for details.
